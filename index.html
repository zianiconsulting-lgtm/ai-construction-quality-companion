What is in the toolkit

  • spreadsheets/ — the main way to prepare the checked tables the chapters give to an AI tool, without programming: for each workflow a step-by-step method with a worked table, and an .xlsx workbook (open this first). Formula CSVs are supplied as an alternative. The workbooks were produced and checked with LibreOffice 26.2 only; other applications were not tested.
  • prompts/ — the ten production prompts, copied from the chapters. Each prompt's status line in the book states its limited test on fictional cases for that version only; none is described as reliable.
  • datasets/ — the fictional Harbour Lane records and practice data used in the chapters (CSV).
  • tools/ — optional Python scripts (standard library only) that build the same checked tables. They decide nothing: a person checks every table before use.
  • README.txt and manifest.csv — what each folder holds, and every file's size and SHA-256.

Fictional data and limits

All people, organisations, records and figures in the toolkit are fictional teaching data. Use them to practise, not as real records or benchmarks.

The toolkit is not professional, legal, engineering or certification advice, and AI output is not a substitute for review by a competent person. Check the legal, contract and project requirements that apply in your jurisdiction and on your project.
